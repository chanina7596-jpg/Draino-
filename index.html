Common questions

Sewer and drain service FAQs

Do you clean main sewer lines?

Yes. Draino clears main sewer lines for homes, businesses, apartment buildings and managed properties.

Do you offer hydro jetting?

Yes. Hydro jetting may be recommended for grease, sludge, recurring buildup or heavily restricted lines.

Can you inspect the sewer with a camera?

Yes. Camera inspection can help identify roots, breaks, offsets, buildup and recurring blockage points.

Do you clean outside drains?

Yes. We service yard drains, driveway drains, patio drains, window-well drains and other exterior drainage lines.
