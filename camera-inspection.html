Frequently asked questions

What property owners ask us.

Call us when the symptoms do not fit neatly into one category. We can help determine the correct service.

Can a camera see through standing water?

Visibility may be limited when the line is blocked or full of water. Clearing the line first often provides a better inspection.

Can you locate the camera underground?

When locating is needed and conditions allow, compatible equipment can help identify the camera head from above ground.

Do you inspect sewers before home purchases?

Yes. A sewer inspection can reveal visible problems that are not part of a standard home inspection.

Will I see the findings?

We explain the visible condition and can discuss the relevant sections with you during the service.
