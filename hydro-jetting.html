Frequently asked questions

What property owners ask us.

Call us when the symptoms do not fit neatly into one category. We can help determine the correct service.

What is the difference between snaking and hydro jetting?

Snaking cuts or opens a blockage. Hydro jetting uses high-flow water to remove more buildup from the pipe walls.

Can hydro jetting remove roots?

Specialized jetting can cut and flush some roots, but a camera inspection may be needed to identify where they entered.

Is hydro jetting safe for older pipes?

It depends on the material and condition. Suspected damage should be evaluated before jetting.

Do you jet commercial lines?

Yes. We serve restaurants, apartments, stores, schools and managed properties.
