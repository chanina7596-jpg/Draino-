Clear answers before you join

Homeowner plan questions.

Does maintenance guarantee I won't have another backup?

No. Regular cleaning helps reduce buildup and the risk of backups, but it cannot fix damaged pipes or eliminate every blockage. Roots, pipe damage, and other underlying issues may need additional work.

What happens if my line backs up between visits?

Contact Draino for service availability. Enrolled members receive $50 off additional main sewer snaking between scheduled visits. Extra calls are not included free. Any additional charges will be explained before work begins.

Are camera inspections or hydro jetting included?

No. This plan covers routine main-line snaking. Camera inspections, hydro jetting, toilet removal, repairs, and other drain lines are quoted separately and require your approval.

What if I don't have a cleanout, or I'm not sure?

Select “No” or “Not sure” on the form. We will discuss your access and confirm whether the standard plan applies before scheduling.

How do I pay and schedule?

Payment is due after each visit. We contact enrolled homeowners around every six months to arrange a convenient service time. We review the full agreement with you before enrollment; this page only collects a request.

Is this available for businesses?

This package is for homeowners. For a restaurant, store, apartment building, or managed property, ask about commercial drain service and a schedule suited to your property.
