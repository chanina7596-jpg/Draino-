Frequently asked questions

Wesley Hills sewer and drain service questions.

Does Draino provide sewer cleaning throughout Wesley Hills?

Yes. Draino serves homes, businesses, and managed properties throughout Wesley Hills and nearby areas including Monsey, Pomona, New City, and Viola.

When should I request a sewer camera inspection?

A camera inspection is useful when a line clogs repeatedly, roots or damage are suspected, or the exact location of a problem is needed before repair planning.

Can hydro jetting help with recurring buildup?

Yes. Hydro jetting can remove grease, sludge, scale, and other buildup when ordinary snaking does not fully clean the pipe walls. The line and access point should be evaluated first.

Do you clean outside drains?

Yes. Draino cleans accessible driveway, yard, patio, window-well, basement, and other exterior drainage lines.
