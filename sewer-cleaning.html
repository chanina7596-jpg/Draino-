Frequently asked questions

What property owners ask us.

Call us when the symptoms do not fit neatly into one category. We can help determine the correct service.

How do I know if the main sewer line is clogged?

Common signs include several fixtures backing up together, gurgling toilets, water rising in a tub or basement floor-drain backup.

Can you remove tree roots?

We can clear many root blockages. A camera inspection may be recommended because roots usually enter through a joint, crack or damaged section.

Do you provide emergency sewer service?

Yes. Draino provides 24/7 sewer and drain service throughout our Rockland County service area.

Should the line be inspected after cleaning?

A camera inspection is useful when backups recur, roots are present or the pipe condition is uncertain.
