Frequently asked questions

What property owners ask us.

Call us when the symptoms do not fit neatly into one category. We can help determine the correct service.

Do you clean street storm drains?

Draino works on private-property drains. Municipal street drains are generally maintained by the local village or town.

Can you clean underground downspout pipes?

Yes, when access and pipe condition allow. These lines commonly collect leaves, roots and sediment.

Should outside drains be cleaned regularly?

Properties with trees, heavy runoff or past flooding may benefit from seasonal or preventive cleaning.

Can you camera-inspect an outside drain?

Sometimes. Access, pipe size, bends and standing water determine whether a useful inspection is possible.
